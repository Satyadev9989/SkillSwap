How SkillSwap Works

Direct peer learning made collaborative, structured, and completely accessible without financial barriers.

01

Share Your Skills

List what you already know—whether it is Web Development, UI/UX, or Java. Define your comfort level and what skills you wish to learn in exchange.

  • Custom skill topic tags
  • Flexible schedule preferences
  • Personal portfolio showcase
02

Find Your Match

Browse through fellow student peers or let our mutual compatibility algorithm pair you with students who want what you teach and teach what you want.

  • Bidirectional interest matching
  • Skill proficiency filtering
  • Direct 1-on-1 swap proposals
03

Learn & Exchange

Schedule interactive sessions, complete hands-on practical exercises together, review code or design files, and build mutual peer endorsements.

  • Real-time code & design pairing
  • Structured milestone roadmaps
  • Verified peer reviews & ratings
